# Audit committee pack - section guide

Each section names its source and owner. Content rules apply to every section: only register figures, attribution of views, no approval language for pending items.

## 1. Summary and decisions requested

- Three to six bullets: results headline, most significant judgement changes, control environment headline, audit status headline.
- List of decisions requested with page or slide reference.
- No figure appears here that does not appear, with its source, in a later section.

## 2. Financial results

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Headline table | Revenue, operating profit or the entity's primary profit measure, net income, EPS if listed, operating cash flow, net debt or net cash; current period, comparative, change, change percentage |
| Basis | Actual, unaudited or reviewed, as applicable; GAAP or IFRS measure versus non-GAAP measure clearly labelled |
| Drivers | Three to five, each quantified only with register figures and labelled Evidenced or Management view |
| Non-GAAP | Reconciliation reference to the earnings release or appendix |
| Outlook | Only if supplied and approved for the committee; label as Forecast |

## 3. Significant judgements and estimates

Columns: Area, Judgement or estimate, Amount at stake, Key assumptions, Sensitivity, Change since last meeting, Status, Auditor view (as communicated), Source paper.

Typical areas: revenue recognition judgements, goodwill and intangible impairment, inventory valuation, credit loss allowances, income tax and uncertain tax positions, provisions and contingencies, business combinations and purchase price allocation, going concern, lease term judgements, share-based payment valuation.

Status values: Concluded by management, Under review, Awaiting auditor view. Sensitivity is taken from the supplied analysis (for example "a 1 percentage point increase in the discount rate reduces headroom by USD X"); never constructed here.

## 4. Accounting and reporting developments

Columns: Pronouncement or matter, Effective date (first affected period for the entity), Expected impact (summary and quantification label from the assessment), Implementation status, Decisions pending.

Include regulator correspondence (for example comment letters) only as supplied, with response status.

## 5. Control environment

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Testing progress | Controls in scope, tested to date, exceptions; as reported by the internal controls outputs |
| Deficiencies | Count by severity as concluded by management (deficiency, significant deficiency, material weakness, or framework equivalent); new, open and closed since last meeting |
| Remediation | Actions complete, retest status, target dates; "remediated" only when retest evidence is concluded effective |
| Certifications | Timeline for management certifications and assessments, where applicable |

## 6. External audit

- Phase and timetable, with next milestones.
- Request completion: percent Accepted plus Provided, late items count.
- Significant findings and management responses.
- Uncorrected misstatements: rollover and iron curtain totals and percentage of the materiality recorded by the user, without a conclusion.
- Fees: audit fees and non-audit services for pre-approval, from user-supplied figures, with service description.
- The auditor's required communications are referenced by document name and date only.

## 7. Internal audit

- Plan completion: audits planned, completed, in progress, deferred, with reasons as stated by internal audit.
- Reports issued in the period with internal audit's own ratings.
- Overdue management actions: count, ageing, owners by role.
- Proposed plan changes for approval.

## 8. Items for committee decision

Format each item:

- **Decision requested** - "The committee is asked to approve ..." or "The committee is asked to note ...".
- **Background** - two to four sentences.
- **Management recommendation** - attributed to the role making it.
- **Supporting documents** - name, version, location.

Never write "approved", "resolved" or draft resolution text. Resolutions and minutes are prepared by the corporate secretary.

## Figure register rules

1. One row per distinct figure; repeated uses reference the same Figure ID.
2. Source must be a file and sheet and cell, or a document and page, or a ledger account and period.
3. Verified - tied to source requires agreement to a source that has itself passed its checks (compiled statements with cross-checks passing, a completed tie-out log, an approved schedule).
4. Derived figures list their input Figure IDs and the formula.
5. Placeholders in the draft show the Figure ID so owners can locate them.
