## Differences to watch

- **Collectibility threshold.** "Probable" means likely to occur under US GAAP and more likely than not under IFRS; a contract can pass under IFRS and fail under US GAAP.
- **Constraint wording.** US GAAP uses "probable" that a significant reversal will not occur; IFRS uses "highly probable". These are generally regarded as aligned in practice, but cite the framework's own wording.
- **Licences.** US GAAP classifies functional versus symbolic intellectual property; IFRS asks whether the licence gives a right to access or a right to use. Conclusions often agree but the analysis path differs.
- **Elections only in US GAAP.** Immaterial promises, shipping and handling as fulfilment, and excluding sales taxes from the transaction price.
- **Reversal of contract cost impairment.** IFRS permits reversal (IFRS 15.104); US GAAP prohibits it (340-40-35-6).
- **Interim and private company relief.** US GAAP has disclosure relief for non-public entities; check the entity type in the Finance Profile.

## Variable consideration worksheet

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Element | Rebate, volume discount, service-level credit, bonus, penalty, price concession, right of return |
| Clause | Contract reference |
| Method | Expected value (many similar contracts or many outcomes) or Most likely amount (binary outcomes) |
| Scenarios | Outcome, probability, source of probability (history, forecast, management estimate) |
| Gross estimate | Expected value = sum of (probability x outcome); most likely = outcome with the highest probability |
| Constraint factors | Susceptibility to external factors, limited experience, long resolution period, broad range of price concessions, many possible outcomes |
| Included amount | Amount for which a significant reversal is not expected under the framework's threshold |
| Status | Concluded - evidenced / Concluded - judgement / Open - information needed |

Probabilities with no documented source are labelled management estimates and listed on the Open Questions sheet for evidence.

## Contract balance roll

For each period: `Closing net contract position = Opening net position + Billings - Revenue recognized`, where a credit net position is a contract liability and a debit net position is a contract asset (or receivable once the right to consideration is unconditional). Present by contract; do not net contract assets and liabilities across contracts.
