# Severity factors, exposure formulas and considerations profiles

Definitions follow PCAOB AS 2201 Appendix A (A3 deficiency, A7 material weakness, A11 significant deficiency) and the SEC's interpretive guidance for management (Release 33-8810). Retrieve authoritative text when exact wording is needed; do not quote at length.

## 1. Deficiency Log columns

Deficiency ID; Source (test workpaper, ITGC test, SoD review, internal audit, external audit, self-identified); Control ID; Key / non-key; Design or operating; Condition; Criterion; Cause (Stated / Evidenced); Effect; Period affected (from, to); Items tested; Exceptions; Observed rate; Accounts or disclosures; Assertions; Process; COSO component and principle; Linked deficiencies (shared root cause); Dependent controls and IPE (for ITGC items); Workpaper status; Source citation.

## 2. Likelihood factors (AS 2201.65)

Record Present / Not present / Unknown with evidence:

| Factor | Examples of evidence |
|---|---|
| Nature of accounts, disclosures and assertions | Estimate, revenue, fraud-susceptible assertion |
| Susceptibility to loss or fraud | Cash, inventory, manual journals |
| Subjectivity, complexity, judgement | Reserves, fair value, complex contracts |
| Interaction with other controls | Interdependent controls failing together; redundant controls operating |
| Interaction among deficiencies | Several deficiencies on the same account or assertion |
| Possible future consequences | Growth in volume, a system change planned, recurring failures |

The assessment can be made without quantifying a probability percentage.

## 3. Magnitude and exposure (AS 2201.66-.67)

- `Gross exposure = recorded balance or total transactions processed through the control during the period affected` (cite the source total).
- Overstatement exposure is capped at the recorded amount; record understatement exposure separately as a narrative consideration when the recorded amount does not bound it.
- Volume: include activity expected in future periods when the deficiency is unremediated at the assessment date.
- `Known misstatement` = errors actually identified (audit adjustments, corrected errors).
- `Projected misstatement (indicative) = (sum of misstatement in sample items / sum of sample item amounts) x population amount`.
- `Exposure ratio = Gross exposure / Overall materiality` (1 dp).
- Group exposure: for deficiencies affecting the same balance, use the largest single exposure on that balance, not the sum, and show both figures.

## 4. Compensating controls (AS 2201.68)

Credit only when all are true and cited:

1. A test workpaper shows the compensating control operated effectively for the same period.
2. It addresses the same account and assertion.
3. Its precision (investigation threshold, disaggregation) would prevent or detect a misstatement that could be material; record `threshold / materiality`.

## 5. Material weakness indicators (AS 2201.69) and the prudent official question (AS 2201.70)

- Fraud, whether or not material, on the part of senior management.
- Restatement of previously issued financial statements to correct a material misstatement.
- Identification by the auditor of a material misstatement in the current period that ICFR would not have detected.
- Ineffective oversight of external financial reporting and ICFR by the audit committee.
- Prudent official question: would officials in the conduct of their own affairs conclude they lack reasonable assurance that transactions are recorded to permit preparation of financial statements in conformity with GAAP? Recorded as a question for management, never answered by the skill.

## 6. Considerations profile rules (not a classification)

Apply to each deficiency and each aggregation group, and show the rule that fired:

| Profile | Fires when |
|---|---|
| Material weakness considerations present | Any MW indicator Present; or at least one likelihood factor Present and gross exposure at or above overall materiality and no compensating control credited; or a pervasive ITGC or entity-level deficiency affecting multiple significant accounts with no credited compensation |
| Significant deficiency considerations present | Not above, and either exposure at or above any significant deficiency benchmark the user supplies, or a key control failure on a significant account where a compensating control is credited but material exposure remains a judgement, or the matter is one the methodology says merits audit committee attention |
| Deficiency considerations only | Neither of the above; exposure below the user's benchmarks or a non-key control with credited compensation |

If materiality or benchmarks are `Not provided`, the profile is "Profile not assessable — threshold missing".

## 7. Management Conclusion sheet

Deficiency or group ID; Considerations profile; Management classification (Deficiency / Significant deficiency / Material weakness); Rationale; Concluded by (name, role); Date; Communicated to (audit committee, auditor) with date; Reference to remediation item. The skill leaves every column except the first two blank until management provides them.
