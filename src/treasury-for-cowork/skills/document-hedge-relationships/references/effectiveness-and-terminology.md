## Terminology map

| Concept | ASC 815 (US GAAP) | IFRS 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Hedge types | Fair value hedge; cash flow hedge; net investment hedge (ASC 815-25, 815-30, 815-35) | Fair value hedge; cash flow hedge; hedge of a net investment in a foreign operation (paragraph 6.5.2) |
| Forecast transaction threshold | Probable | Highly probable (paragraph 6.3.3) |
| Effectiveness requirement | Highly effective, prospectively and retrospectively; a range of 80 to 125 percent is commonly applied in practice | Economic relationship exists; credit risk does not dominate value changes; hedge ratio consistent with risk management (paragraph 6.4.1(c)) |
| Bright-line range | No range in the codification text; practice range adopted in policy | None |
| Deferred amounts (cash flow hedge) | Accumulated other comprehensive income | Cash flow hedge reserve (within equity, through OCI) |
| Ineffectiveness in cash flow hedges | Not separately measured when highly effective; entire change in instrument included in assessment goes to OCI | Measured by the lower-of test; excess in profit or loss (paragraph 6.5.11) |
| Forecast purchase of a non-financial asset | Reclassify from AOCI when the hedged item affects earnings | Basis adjustment to the asset's initial carrying amount (paragraph 6.5.11(d)(i)) |
| Items outside the assessment | Excluded components (for example forward points, option time value, cross-currency basis), recognized by amortization or through earnings | Costs of hedging (paragraphs 6.5.15 and 6.5.16) |
| Adjusting the relationship | De-designate and re-designate | Rebalancing (paragraph 6.5.5) |
| Voluntary discontinuation | Permitted | Not permitted while the risk management objective is unchanged (paragraph 6.5.6) |

| Disclosures | ASC 815-10-50 | IFRS 7 paragraphs 21A to 24G |

## Quantitative techniques

**Dollar offset (cumulative).**
`Ratio = |Cumulative change in fair value of hedging instrument| ÷ |Cumulative change in fair value (or present value of cash flows) of hedged item or hypothetical derivative|`.
Check that the changes are in opposite directions; same-direction changes fail regardless of ratio. Small absolute changes can distort the ratio; report the absolute amounts beside it and let the policy's small-change rule, if any, apply.

**Hypothetical derivative (cash flow hedges).**
A derivative whose terms match the critical terms of the hedged item (amount, timing, index, currency) and which has a fair value of zero at inception. Its change in value proxies the hedged item (ASC 815-30; IFRS 9 paragraph B6.5.5). Values come from the valuation provider or the user.

**Regression.**
Regress changes in the instrument on changes in the hedged item over a stated history. Report R-squared, slope and statistical significance against the policy's criteria (commonly R-squared of at least 0.80 and a slope between −0.80 and −1.25 in practice; use the policy's criteria).

**Qualitative (critical terms match).**
Record each critical term for the instrument and the hedged item side by side (notional, currency, maturity or settlement date, underlying, pricing date) and whether counterparty credit standing has changed. Any mismatch moves the assessment to a quantitative method.

## IFRS 9 lower-of test for cash flow hedges

`Reserve balance = lesser of |cumulative gain or loss on instrument from inception| and |cumulative change in fair value of hedged item from inception|`. `Ineffectiveness in profit or loss = cumulative instrument change − reserve balance` (an over-hedge produces ineffectiveness; an under-hedge does not).

## Assessment result labels

| Label | Rule |
|---|---|
| Within range | Quantitative result meets policy criteria |
| Outside range | Quantitative result fails policy criteria |
| Qualitative support documented | Qualitative method applied and critical terms still match |
| Not assessed | Required valuation or hedged item data missing |
