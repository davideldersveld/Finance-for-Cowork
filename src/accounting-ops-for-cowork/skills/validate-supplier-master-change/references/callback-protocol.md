# Out-of-band call-back protocol

## Pass conditions (all required)

| # | Condition | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Number sourced from the trusted baseline (signed contract, onboarding file, master record before the lookback window) | Number came from the request, its signature, an attachment, a link, a website reached from the email, or a recent master change |
| C2 | Number not changed within the lookback window | Phone field edited in the window |
| C3 | Outbound call initiated by company staff | Supplier called in, or a call was "arranged" by the requester |
| C4 | Person reached is a known baseline contact, or reached through the supplier's main switchboard on file | Unknown person on a direct line supplied with the request |
| C5 | Supplier states the new account details and effective date unprompted; last 4 and bank name agree with the request | Staff read out the new details and the supplier said "yes" |
| C6 | Supplier confirms the request originated from them and names the sender | Supplier unaware of the request |
| C7 | Caller name, date, time, number dialled and its source recorded | Undocumented call |
| C8 | Caller independent of the person who received the request, where policy requires | Same person received, verified and entered |

Email confirmation, a reply from the same thread, a text message or a portal message never satisfies the call-back.

## Call-back script (draft for the caller)

1. Dial the number from the trusted baseline only; record the source.
2. Ask for the known contact by name, or ask the switchboard for accounts receivable.
3. "We received a request regarding your payment details. Can you tell me what change you requested and when?"
4. "Please read me the new account holder name, bank name and the last four digits of the account."
5. "From what date should it apply, and who at your company sent the request?"
6. Do not read out the new details first; do not confirm or deny what the request contains.
7. Record the answers, the time, and your name. If anything differs, stop and escalate.

## Documentary evidence checks

- Account holder name equals the supplier's legal name exactly.
- Bank country consistent with the supplier's registered country or prior bank, or a documented business reason.
- Bank letter dated, on letterhead, with a contact that can be checked independently.
- Account-name validation result, where the user supplies one, shows a full match.
Documents support the call-back; they never replace it.
